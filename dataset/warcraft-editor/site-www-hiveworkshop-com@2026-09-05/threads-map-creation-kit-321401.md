 Introduction:

 Hello. Im here to drop this super duper map creation kit that I made, which contains tools to create full HD up to 4K LOADING SCREENS and MAP ICONS, tools to PROTECT your maps from filthy thieves, and even maps with TRIGGERS that can be easily copied and pasted to your maps .

 This kit contains 4 folders: LoadingScreen, MapIcon, Protect and Triggers. Each having a tutorial.txt or a readme.txt explaining how each procedure goes. Its super simple to follow, and doesn't requires much time!

 There are 2 programs in this kit, one used to create .blp images for your loading screens, and another used to protect your maps .
