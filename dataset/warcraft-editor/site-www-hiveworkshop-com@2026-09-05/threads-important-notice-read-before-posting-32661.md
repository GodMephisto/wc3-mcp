Welcome to Hive Workshop's General Mapping Tutorials!

This forum is open for posting so that users may comment on the content, information and style of approved tutorials. Posts must directly relate to the tutorials themselves.

Rules

Global site rules apply.

Try to keep comments/criticism elaborate and helpful.

Questions regarding general mapping matters, that are not explained in the tutorial, should be asked in World Editor Help Zone.

You can't create new threads in here. All new tutorials must be posted in the Tutorial Submission forum and will be moved to its respective subforum by a moderator if approved.
