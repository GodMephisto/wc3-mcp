## Description

Welcome to Turquoise Chronicles

Devlog #1 Now we are getting progress.

I'm a solo developer building an isometric turn based tactics game. Grounded, medieval, and low fantasy settings. In this first devlog I share the dream behind the project, what I'm making, why, and where I want to take it. Full commitment, the honest start of a long journey.

At its core is a turn system I'm calling Discipline: the more disciplined your unit, the later it commits to a plan, but the sooner it strikes. Every round is a gamble of information and timing.

Follow the journey, new devlogs as the game grows every Monday,
