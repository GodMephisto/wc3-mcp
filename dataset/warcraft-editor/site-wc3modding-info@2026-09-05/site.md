 Jetcraft

JetCraft is a 2D platform sidescroller for StarCraft 2 with its own level editor.

The player's objective is to collect all the golden keys and leave through the level exit.
The exit will unlock once all the keys scattered around the level has been acquired.
The player will also be able to collect various treasures to increase the end score, but only the keys are mandatory to complete the levels.

JetCraft comes with its own level editor which allows users to create their own custom levels to play and share with other players.
