2. Wirt's other leg
In the last mission of the Alliance campaign - Chapter 6 - you can slay Diablo's The Butcher (an abomination)  who drops an item called "wirt's other leg". This is a reference to Blizzard's own Diablo 2 item "Wirt's leg" which in turn is a reference to the boy Wirt from Diablo. The item itself is useless in The Frozen Throne.

3. Villagers turned Zombie
In "March of the Scourge " there is a farm with three villagers at the bottom of the map very near the western entrance who will turn into Zombies when approached. 

4. Fight Club:
In "Trudging through the Ashes", After you get your shade, go look inside the Main Town. You will see two civilians fighting with a large crowd watching. They are named Robert and Tyler. These were the names from the two guys from the Movie Fight Club. If you kill one of them, the other one will run away.

Storm-wind. It will create a Protective Aura around your Hero.

6. Ancient Figurine
In "The Long March", After reaching the first oasis look a little bit above it and there should be a few murlocs and 4 murloc huts. 2 of them drop items. One drops an Ancient Figurine and the other drops a Potion Of Mana.
