*Description* - This campaign is made by TheKillerKow and is full of comedic elements. In this, the Mad Wizard Bobius Bobman and his Sheep Army must travel to Stone Hoofpeak for a meeting with his therapist. 

*Thoughts* - Man, whoever made this campaign was on drugs or something, because the cutscenes are so weird and crazy lmao. I love it though, these comedy campaigns are pretty rare so we should cherish them as much as we can. This mission is pretty much a fun micro mission with 2 equally fun side quests, but I will say this map in particular had some problems.
- Shaun's second ability says one thing in the ability menu and another in the normal menu, I have still no clue what it does. Turns out you can autocast it and it drains mana? No idea how it works.
- There is a barracks at the beginning which can upgrade 2 abilities but it requires other buildings to be built first, and we can't build other buildings on this map so why is it there?
- The terrain especially near the middle part of the map with the green dragons could use a lot of work as unit pathing was super weird. Also the part near the circle has a weird pathing blocker that prevents you from going in an area for no reason.
- What's with the random gold and lumber pickups I wonder? Maybe we will use them in the mission coming after?
- I am not a fan of the way the secret on this map is locked behind you having to get enough XP on Bob to get Flame Strike, because you get BARELY enough XP for him to get him to Level 3, and also the only way to get him to Level 3 is to not accidentally do the middle portion of the map first without him (which I did in the beginning part of the video), so I had to replay the whole mission to get flame strike for the secret.

Having said all the negatives though, this map is pretty great. I loved the secret area, although I don't think finding the mushroom cave did anything. I did love the weird mushroom enemy fight though lol, and the drops look pretty interesting. I have to say, I commend the ideas this map-maker had building this mission lol. Defeating Ranger Rick and Smokey the Bear was pretty funny. Oh also, I LOVED the parody on Arthas waiting for Jaina to come in Human 03 in the beginning, and how this time Footman Bill wanted the help but noone helped him XD I am really enjoying this weird psychedelic journey haha.
